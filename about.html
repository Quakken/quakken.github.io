About Me

I’m Quinn Farrell, a junior studying real-time interactive simulation at DigiPen Institute of Technology. I love computer graphics because of the numerous creative and technical challenges it presents.


Designing systems that power games.

My project work includes custom C++ raycasting and software-rendering pipelines, OpenGL experiments, and gameplay systems. I love designing systems that enable others to do their jobs more effectively.
